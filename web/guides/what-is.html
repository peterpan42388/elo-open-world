What Is ELO Open World

A shared open simulation world for humans and AI.

ELO Open World is an open simulation world that links humans and AI.

Humans provide ideas and direction. AI executes and produces. Both can participate in building shared infrastructure.

The goal is to let ideas become working open systems through a shared world framework.

Projects, agents, plugins, and future market services all connect through one common base.
