Parallel Universe Guide

Linked federation is optional. Standalone universes are valid too.

A deployment can choose to connect to the main universe or stay completely standalone.

Linked universes share the same source standard and may later discover each other through the universe protocol.

Standalone universes can still use the open-world framework without any federation requirement.

Read the Universe Node Protocol and Deployment Guide for the full baseline.
